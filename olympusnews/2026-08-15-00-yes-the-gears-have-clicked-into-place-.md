Spark in the Machine is one of the most ambitious standalones of all time: ABuffZucchini & Ricky06 poured 3 years of work into 100+ rooms, custom gimmicks, and a ridiculous number of assets. Intricate contraptions & machinery are weaved with movement that is sharp as steel. Not to mention the 45 minutes of custom music by Soggy Milk. Do yourself (and Mr. Oshiro) a favor by investigating this map in the link below!
