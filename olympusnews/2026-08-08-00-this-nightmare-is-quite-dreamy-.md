In the works for nearly 2 years, The Nightmare features 17 maps all made by 1 person, Shinxray! Inspired by Pokemon Mystery Dungeon Explorers of Sky, this pack started out as a university art project! Shinxray began weaving in a number of different inspirations, including other games, music, and paintings, resulting in a wide variety of maps! Close your eyes and prepare to be frightened by the creativity on display!
