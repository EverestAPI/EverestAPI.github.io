1. Dream in Starry Skies and 2. Beiping Post, Chronicle & Bulletin are 2 different standalones where 2 separate sets of mappers, both from the Chinese modding community, contributed rooms to each! Both maps center Air Dream Blocks, a dream block where you can choose to dash through the block or completely pass through it. However, the surrounding secondary gimmicks & the overall gameplay style is completely different! Both maps peek a bit into green grandmaster but are mostly easier.
